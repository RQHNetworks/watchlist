**Data & Method**
- **Fiscal-year labels:** CrowdStrike's fiscal year ends Jan 31, so "FY2027" = Feb 2026–Jan 2027; "Q2 FY27" is the quarter ended Jul 31, 2026, reported Aug 26, 2026. All bars in Panels 1/2 sit at the calendar month each quarter was actually REPORTED (SEC 8-K Item 2.02 filing date), not fiscal quarter-end — cadence drifts year to year (e.g. Q2: Aug 30 '23 → Aug 28 '24 → Aug 27 '25 → Aug 26 '26).
- **Stock split:** CRWD executed a 4-for-1 split effective Jul 2, 2026 (announced Jun 3, 2026). All price history (Panels 1 & 3) is already split-adjusted by Yahoo Finance. Panel 4 EPS is normalized to a consistent POST-split basis throughout: Q3 FY26, Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 were originally reported pre-split and have been divided by 4 here so all eight bars are directly comparable to the post-split Q2 FY27 actual.
- **FCF basis:** FY2025–FY2027 quarterly free cash flow is CrowdStrike's own reported discrete-quarter figure from each earnings release. FY2022–FY2024 quarters are not individually headlined in those older releases, so the discrete quarter is computed from SEC XBRL company facts (operating cash flow less purchases of property & equipment, YTD-differenced) and then scaled so all four quarters in each fiscal year tie exactly to CrowdStrike's own reported ANNUAL free cash flow (the two bases differ by ~4–5%, mainly capitalized internal-use software cash outflows the PP&E-only XBRL tag excludes).
- **Non-GAAP substitution:** Panel 4 uses non-GAAP (comparable) diluted EPS throughout, not GAAP, since GAAP is persistently distorted by stock-based compensation — standard practice for this company and consistent with how consensus estimates are quoted.
- **Comparability:** nothing breaks the year-over-year comparisons charted here. CrowdStrike's July 19, 2024 global outage (in the Q2 FY25 quarter) depressed free cash flow for several following quarters (management cited roughly \$61M of drag in Q1 FY26) but is not a spin-off/divestiture-type break, so no quarter was excluded; the dip is visible in the FCF bars themselves rather than papered over.
- **Confirmed vs estimated.** Confirmed: all 21 reported revenue and FCF quarters (SEC XBRL / CrowdStrike press releases); all four EPS actuals and the consensus each beat (Zacks/MarketBeat-sourced, cross-checked against each 8-K); all 55 weekly closes; both SMA series, which reconcile exactly to wallstreetnumbers.com's dated readings (50D \$203.41 / 200D \$144.67 on Sep 14, 2026, both 1-year highs; 50D 1-yr low \$101.29 Apr 16 '26; 200D 1-yr low \$103.18 Sep 15 '25; year-end 2025 \$128.27 / \$115.31). Estimated: Q3/Q4 FY27 revenue is implied by the raised \$5.99–6.01B FY27 guide less H1 actual, split by the prior year's Q3/Q4 seasonality; **Q1/Q2 FY28 revenue is model-derived** (no FY28 guidance or broker quarterly consensus exists yet), assuming decelerating ~20–22% YoY growth; **all four forward FCF bars are model-derived** from estimated revenue times the prior year's same-quarter FCF margin, trending toward management's ≥30% FY27 / 34–38% FY29 margin targets; **Q3/Q4 FY27 EPS estimates** split the raised \$1.25–1.26 (post-split) FY27 EPS guide less H1 actual by the prior year's seasonality; **Q1/Q2 FY28 EPS is model-derived** off FY27 actuals and CrowdStrike's recent EPS-growth cadence — no broker publishes a quarterly EPS consensus this far out. Forward release months are projected from CrowdStrike's own cadence (Q1: ~Jun 2–4; Q2: ~Aug 26–30; Q3: ~Nov 26–Dec 2; Q4: ~Mar 3–7).

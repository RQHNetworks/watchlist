- **Fiscal-year labels:** none needed. Astera Labs' fiscal year is the calendar year, so "Q2 2026" means the quarter ended Jun 30, 2026 on every source cited here.

Astera Labs' non-GAAP diluted EPS excludes stock-based compensation and acquisition-related items; headline GAAP EPS would distort the beat/miss read given the company's heavy SBC load, and non-GAAP is also the basis sell-side consensus is quoted against for this name.

- **Confirmed vs estimated.** Confirmed: all ten quarterly revenue and FCF actuals and all four EPS actuals and their consensus estimates (company press releases, SEC filings, Zacks/company reporting); all 55 weekly closes; both SMA series, which reconcile to the cent against every dated reading wallstreetnumbers.com publishes (50D \$315.53 / 200D \$228.70 on Sep 14, 2026; 50D 1-yr high \$357.82 Aug 7 '26 and low \$129.41 Apr 10 '26; 200D 1-yr high \$228.70 today and low \$107.98 Sep 15 '25).

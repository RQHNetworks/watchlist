**Data & Method**
- **Fiscal-year labeling:** Coherent's fiscal year ends June 30, so every quarter label here is fiscal, not calendar (e.g. "Q4 FY26" = quarter ended Jun 30, 2026, reported Aug 12, 2026).
- **Merger break in comparability:** the II-VI / Coherent Inc. merger closed Jul 1, 2022; Q1 FY23 (reported Nov 9, 2022) is the first combined-company quarter, and revenue roughly jumped from consolidation, not organic growth. Every FY22-vs-FY23 same-quarter comparison is therefore excluded from any growth-rate statement in this file and the chart (Q1-Q4 FY23 bars are still plotted as real reported figures, just never diffed against the legacy II-VI-only bars before them).
- **FCF basis:** revenue and FCF are stockanalysis.com's discrete, already quarter-differenced figures (operating cash flow less capex, computed from the cumulative 10-Q cash-flow statements). Verified against Coherent's own FY2026 full-year press-release totals (OCF \$79.5M, capex \$1,102.9M, FCF -\$1,023.4M), which tie to the sum of the four FY26 quarterly bars to the decimal.
- **Non-GAAP substitution:** Panel 4 uses non-GAAP diluted EPS throughout (Coherent's own preferred metric), which strips merger-related and restructuring charges that would otherwise swing several quarters to a GAAP loss and make beat/miss meaningless (e.g. Q4 FY26 GAAP EPS \$1.19 vs non-GAAP \$1.74).
- **Consensus source:** the Zacks-consensus series is used for all four reported quarters because its "actual" EPS matches Coherent's own reported non-GAAP figure in every quarter; other vendors (e.g. MarketBeat) publish different consensus numbers for the same quarters, though the sign of the surprise agrees across every source checked.
- **Confirmed vs. estimated:** all 20 actual revenue/FCF quarters and all 4 actual EPS quarters are confirmed against SEC filings, Coherent press releases and earnings-call transcripts. Forward Q1 FY27 revenue (\$2.30B) and EPS (\$1.95) use the company's own Aug 12, 2026 guidance midpoints; **Q2-Q4 FY27 revenue and EPS are model-derived**, splitting the \$10.62B / \$9.42 FY27 analyst consensus (stockanalysis.com) across the remaining three quarters on a trajectory consistent with management's stated goal of exiting FY27 above a \$3B quarterly revenue run rate. **All four forward FCF bars are model-derived** — no broker publishes a quarterly FCF consensus for COHR — built from the guided sequential capex ramp (capex \$556M in Q4 FY26, guided higher again in Q1 FY27) against a gradually recovering operating cash flow as the AI-optics inventory build normalizes.
- **SMA cross-check:** the computed 50-day/200-day SMA reconcile to the cent with wallstreetnumbers.com's dated Sep 14, 2026 readings (50D \$299.16, 200D \$280.25; 50D 1-year low \$98.60 and 200D 1-year low \$85.88, both Sep 15, 2025).
- **Sources:** SEC EDGAR (8-K/10-Q filings), Coherent Corp press releases and earnings-call transcripts (Nov 5 '25, Feb 4 '26, May 6 '26, Aug 12 '26 releases), stockanalysis.com, wallstreetnumbers.com, Zacks/Yahoo Finance/StreetInsider earnings coverage, Seeking Alpha, and Yahoo Finance daily closes via yfinance.

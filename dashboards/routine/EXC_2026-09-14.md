**Data & Method**
- **Fiscal year:** Exelon's fiscal year is the calendar year, so "Q2 2026" means the quarter ended Jun 30, 2026 throughout.
- **Comparability across the Constellation spinoff:** Exelon spun off its Generation business as Constellation Energy (CEG) on Feb 1, 2022, but Exelon had already been reporting Generation as discontinued operations ahead of the split — so the SEC XBRL figures used here (`Revenues`, `NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities`, `PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment`) are on a continuing-operations (utility-only) basis all the way back to Q1 2021. No quarter needed to be excluded for comparability.
- **FCF basis:** Exelon's cash-flow statement reports operating cash flow and capex as year-to-date cumulative, never as a discrete quarter, so every bar in Panel 2 is YTD-differenced from SEC XBRL company facts (CIK 0001109357) and, for Q1–Q2 2026, the Q2 2026 10-Q (filed 2026-07-30) cash-flow statement. E.g. Q2 2026 = \$3,669M H1 OCF − \$1,724M Q1 OCF = \$1,945M, less (\$4,558M − \$2,358M) = \$2,200M capex → −\$255M FCF.
- **Non-GAAP substitution:** Panel 4 uses Exelon's own headline "adjusted (non-GAAP) operating earnings per share," which strips mark-to-market and other one-time items and is the basis both management's guidance and the Zacks consensus are quoted on — GAAP EPS at a regulated utility can otherwise swing on items unrelated to the operating trend.
- **Confirmed vs. estimated/modeled:** Confirmed — all 22 actual revenue/OCF/capex quarters (SEC XBRL, 10-Q R-files); all four EPS actuals and their Zacks consensus estimates (Q3'25 \$0.86 vs \$0.76, Q4'25 \$0.59 vs \$0.53, Q1'26 \$0.91 vs \$0.89, Q2'26 \$0.43 vs \$0.43); monthly/weekly closes and 52-week high/low (yfinance); the 50D/200D SMA current values, which reconcile exactly to the trigger's stated \$45.04 / \$45.06 on a dividend-adjusted-close basis. Modeled — Q3'26/Q4'26 EPS (\$0.90 / \$0.62) split the FY26 Zacks consensus (\$2.86) using Exelon's FY25 Q3/Q4 seasonal weighting, since no single-quarter consensus is published yet ~7 weeks ahead of the print; Q1'27/Q2'27 EPS (\$0.97 / \$0.46) split the FY27 consensus (\$3.04) the same way. All four forward revenue quarters are modeled at a flat +7% YoY (between H1 2026's realized +8.8% and the company's longer-run ~6% rate-base algorithm), and all four forward FCF quarters are modeled from the company's ~\$10.0B FY26 total-capex commentary and the \$41.7B 2026–29 four-year plan (~\$10.4B/yr for FY27), against OCF grown at the same +7% YoY — Exelon does not guide quarterly revenue or FCF, and no broker publishes a quarterly FCF consensus for it.
- **Price basis:** Panel 3's weekly close and SMA lines use dividend-adjusted close specifically because that is the basis that reconciles to the trigger's exact death-cross values; wallstreetnumbers.com's own live page shows \$45.42 / \$45.88 for the same date on an unadjusted-close basis (Exelon's Sep 4, 2026 ex-dividend date explains the gap) — same crossover, same story, different price basis. Panel 1's long-run chart and the 52-week high/low use unadjusted close / intraday range, the standard quoting convention.
- **Consensus dispersion:** For Q2 2026, Zacks called the \$0.43 print "in line" against a \$0.43 consensus, while at least one other outlet cited a \$0.48 Street estimate and called it a miss, and a pre-print preview cited \$0.45 — Panel 4 uses the Zacks series throughout all four reported quarters for a single, internally consistent source.

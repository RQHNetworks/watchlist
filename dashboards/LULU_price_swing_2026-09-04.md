**Data & Method**
- **Fiscal-year labels** use lululemon's own convention (FY2025 ended Feb 1, 2026; FY2026 ends Jan 31, 2027). Most vendors label the same quarters one year higher — their "Q2 2027" is FY26 Q2 here. FY23 Q4 was a 14-week quarter in a 53-week fiscal year, which inflates that bar against its neighbours.
- **FCF basis:** lululemon publishes only year-to-date cash-flow statements, never a discrete quarterly one, so every bar is YTD-differenced (operating cash flow less purchases of property and equipment) straight from the company's SEC XBRL facts. E.g. FY26 Q2 = \$589.3M H1 OCF − \$214.4M Q1 OCF = \$374.8M, less \$149.7M capex = \$225.2M. That \$225.2M includes the \$134.5M tariff refund; underlying FCF was roughly \$87M.
- **Release months** are the SEC 8-K Item 2.02 filing dates, checked individually — the cadence is not fixed for a given fiscal quarter across years. Q4 moved Mar 29 '22 → Mar 28 '23 → Mar 21 '24 → Mar 27 '25 → Mar 17 '26; Q2 moved Sep 8 '21 → Aug 31 '23 / Aug 29 '24 → Sep 4 '25 / Sep 3 '26.
- **Non-GAAP substitution (Panel 4):** FY26 Q2 headline diluted EPS was \$2.92, but \$0.86/sh (net of tax) came from one-time IEEPA tariff refunds of \$134.5M plus interest. The comparable \$2.06 is charted, so the quarter reads as a +\$0.27 beat rather than a meaningless +\$1.13 against a consensus that never contemplated the refund.
- **Confirmed vs estimated.** Confirmed: all revenue, FCF and EPS actuals; all four consensus estimates (\$2.22 Zacks for FY25 Q3, \$4.78 for FY25 Q4, \$1.68 LSEG for FY26 Q1, \$1.79 LSEG for FY26 Q2); all 53 weekly closes; both SMA series, which reconcile exactly to wallstreetnumbers.com's dated readings (50D \$118.40, 200D \$152.77 on Sep 4, 2026; 50D 1-yr low \$117.57 on Aug 17, 2026; 1-yr highs 50D \$214.25 / 200D \$301.56 on Sep 4, 2025). Estimated: FY26 Q3 revenue/EPS are the company guidance midpoints; FY26 Q4 is implied by the FY26 guide less H1 actual less the Q3 guide; **FY27 Q1/Q2 revenue and EPS are model-derived** (published FY27 quarterly consensus still sits on pre-cut assumptions and is being revised down); **all four forward FCF bars are model-derived** — no broker publishes a quarterly FCF consensus.
- **Comparability:** no acquisition, divestiture or spin-off breaks year-over-year comparability in this window; the MIRROR / lululemon Studio wind-down was immaterial to reported revenue, so no growth rate here is distorted by a structural change.

**Data & Method**
- **Only six reported quarters exist.** CoreWeave IPO'd Mar 28, 2025 at \$40.00, so Panels 1 and 2 are empty from Jan 2021 to Mar 2025 by construction, not by omission. The 2024 quarterly figures do exist in the S-1 and as 10-Q comparatives, but they were never *released* as quarterly results, so plotting them on a release-month axis would be false — they are excluded.
- **Fiscal-year labels:** none needed. CoreWeave's fiscal year is the calendar year, so "Q2 2026" means the quarter ended Jun 30, 2026 on every source cited here.
- **FCF basis:** CoreWeave publishes only cumulative year-to-date cash-flow statements, never a discrete quarterly one, so every bar is YTD-differenced from SEC XBRL company facts — `NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities` less `PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment`. E.g. Q2 2026 = \$3,663M H1 OCF − \$2,984M Q1 OCF = \$679M, less (\$14,117M − \$7,695M) = \$6,422M capex → −\$5,743M. All six computed bars tie to stockanalysis.com's independently derived quarterly FCF to the last decimal. Note the press-release "capex" line is not always the cash figure (Q3 2025 press release: \$3,278.8M including capitalised internal-use software vs \$2,389.2M of cash PP&E purchases); the cash basis is used throughout for consistency.
- **Non-GAAP substitution: none.** No one-time item distorts any of the four beat/miss comparisons — CoreWeave's adjustments are recurring stock-based comp and financing items — so Panel 4 charts the diluted net loss per share straight from each 8-K. For reference, the company's own adjusted EPS was −\$0.08, −\$0.56, −\$1.12 and −\$1.03 for those same four quarters.
- **Consensus dispersion is real here.** Vendors disagree on the basis: for Q2 2026 the estimate CRWV beat is quoted as −\$1.52 (MarketBeat), −\$1.41 (Investing.com/LSEG) and −\$1.17 (Zacks). Panel 4 uses the MarketBeat series because its "actual" column matches CoreWeave's own reported diluted EPS for all four quarters, making estimate and actual internally consistent. The *sign* of the surprise is the same across every source for all four quarters.
- **Comparability:** nothing breaks the year-over-year comparison. The \$9B Core Scientific acquisition was terminated Oct 30, 2025 after CORZ shareholders voted it down, so it never entered the financials; Weights & Biases (closed May 2025) and the later tuck-ins were immaterial to reported revenue.
- **Confirmed vs estimated.** Confirmed: all six revenue and FCF actuals (SEC XBRL); all four EPS actuals (8-K press releases); all 54 weekly closes; both SMA series, which reconcile exactly to every dated reading wallstreetnumbers.com publishes (50D \$85.68 / 200D \$91.60 on Sep 8, 2026; 50D 1-yr high \$123.43 Nov 10 '25 and low \$80.59 Jan 23 '26; 200D 1-yr high \$110.07 Mar 4 '26 and low \$91.35 Sep 1 '26; year-end 2025 \$91.49 / \$103.41). Estimated: Q3 2026 revenue is the \$3.45–3.6B guidance midpoint; **Q4 2026 revenue is implied** by the raised \$12.4–13.2B FY26 guide less H1 actual less the Q3 guide; **Q1/Q2 2027 revenue is model-derived**, splitting the \$26.47B FY27 consensus; **all four forward FCF bars are model-derived** from guided capex (Q3 2026 \$11.5–13.5B; Q4 2026 the residual of the \$35–39B FY26 guide) — no broker publishes a quarterly FCF consensus. Forward release months are projected from CoreWeave's own cadence, which drifts (Q1: May 14 '25 → May 7 '26; Q2: Aug 12 '25 → Aug 11 '26); actuals use SEC 8-K Item 2.02 filing dates.
